# AI Tools Hub: Academic & Research AI Intelligence Platform

Maintained by the **Prototyping Research Center (PRC)** under the auspices of the **University AI Committee (UAC)**.

An empirical, research-driven intelligence database cataloguing **215 verified AI tools** across **15 operational categories**. Designed specifically for academic scholars, laboratory researchers, software engineers, and graduate students.

---

## 🌟 Key Platform Highlights

- **215 Verified Tools**: Exhaustive metadata, real-world pricing models, verification benchmark dates, and direct documentation links.
- **150 "Hidden Gems"**: High-utility, low-cost or open-source specialized research tools.
- **41 Chinese AI Frontier Models**: DeepSeek (V3 & R1), Qwen 2.5 suite, Kimi, GLM-4, Wan 2.1, Kling AI, Hailuo AI, MinerU, ModelScope.
- **141 Open Source & Local Tools**: Hardware-specified runtimes (Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM, LM Studio, ComfyUI, whisper.cpp) enabling zero-marginal-cost, private execution.
- **61 Hugging Face Ecosystem Picks**: Kokoro-82M, Florence-2, BGE-M3, Surya OCR, Docling, SAM 2, SmolLM2.
- **119 Tools for Research & Science**: Systematic review screening (ASReview LAB, Rayyan), citation networks (ResearchRabbit, Inciteful, Litmaps), and scientific QA (PaperQA, Consensus, SciSpace).
- **Semantic Intent Search**: Real-time client-side natural language query parser handling queries like *"free AI tool for literature review"*, *"cheap alternative to Midjourney"*, or *"chinese video AI"*.
- **Curated Discovery Collections**: Instant one-click filtering across 9 live collections.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Engine**: Compare up to 4 tools across hardware requirements, international accessibility, developer APIs, and cheapest legitimate routes.
- **Standalone Architecture**: Single-file, zero-dependency HTML/CSS/JS with embedded SVG and visual assets.

## 📂 Categories Covered

1. **AI for Research & Science** (`Rs`): Literature reviews, evidence synthesis, citations, and bioinformatics
2. **AI Coding & Dev Agents** (`Cd`): Autonomous terminal agents, in-editor assistants, PR reviewers
3. **Open Source & Local AI** (`Os`): Local inference runtimes, quantization engines, and open weights
4. **AI Image & Visuals** (`Im`): Latent diffusion, FLUX.1, diagramming, and graphic synthesis
5. **AI Audio & Voice** (`Au`): Speech recognition, neural TTS, voice cloning, and transcription
6. **Chinese AI Frontier** (`Ch`): Frontier reasoning LLMs, multimodal models, and domestic model hubs
7. **AI Agents & Frameworks** (`Ag`): Browser automation, multi-agent collaboration, and execution graphs
8. **AI Video & Motion** (`Vd`): Video diffusion, camera motion, and avatar synthesis
9. **AI Writing & Reasoning** (`Wr`): Academic drafting, reasoning, style correction, and grammar
10. **AI Data & Computation** (`Da`): Statistics, computational notebooks, and bioinformatics
11. **Workflow Automation** (`At`): Self-hosted automation, API webhook pipelines, and orchestration
12. **AI Productivity** (`Pr`): Workspace knowledge bases, meeting transcription, and notes
13. **AI Design** (`Ds`): Academic posters, vector graphics, and interface prototyping
14. **AI Presentation** (`Ps`): Slide decks, conference presentations, and thesis defences
15. **AI Education** (`Ed`): Pedagogical AI, Socratic tutoring, and study assistance

## 🚀 Deployment

This application is built as a zero-dependency standalone web app. It can be served directly via any static hosting service or GitHub Pages:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/dto992447-dev/ai-tools-hub.git

# Open locally
open index.html
```

Or deploy automatically using **GitHub Pages** (Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from a branch: `main` / `root`).
